FRO/RRO

We are informing to all International students that filling and processing of application is ON LINE for Registration for Registration Certificate (RC) & Residential permit (RP) on arrival in lndia, visa Extension for stay visa, Residence permit Extension (RP Extension), change of address, change of course & change of Institution and OFF LINE for No Objection Certificate (NOC) for travelling outside India in Foreigner’s Registration Office (FRO) in Bhopal and Foreigner’s Regional Registration Office (FRRO) in Bhopal. 


Admissions and Facilitation Department of RKDF University is providing facility for filling of documents ON LINE and OFF LINE forms in FRO/ FRRO, For blank processing form, please come to FRO section of Admissions & Facilitation Department.


 It is the prime responsibility of the student to visit Admissions and Facilitation Department in time to complete the required documentations, so that no fine is levied against him/her.

Registration for RC / RP on arrival in India

Processing for Appointment for Registration of New Visa is ON LINE in FRO/FRRO and all processing & submission of documents including taking appointment date should be within 14 days of arrival in India. It is the responsibility of the student to report in time in Admission & Facilitation Cell for filling the papers ON LINE. If he/she fails to file the application in time, he/she is liable to pay fine to Government of India. In case fine is payable, the student will collect the document called “Challan” from the concerned FRO/FRRO to pay fine in Bhopal.

After collection of your RP and RC from FRO/FRRO students are required to submit a copy immediately to Admissions & Facilitation Department of RKDF University.
     Documents required: 
          i. Photograph - 02 nos. 
         ii. Passport & Visa Copy + Original 
        iii. Copy of House Rent agreement 
        iv. C Form for Hostellers 
         v. Copy of House owner’s Identity Proof (ID) and Electricity bill of the Flat / House. 
        vi. Bonafide Certificate - The student will come to FRO Section in Admission & Facilitation Department for the form. The student will fill the form & take signatures of the concerned officers in the FRO Section, which he will submit in the office of his school for getting Bonafide Certificate.

NOC for travelling outside India

 Students are supposed to take “NOC for Travelling outside India” from their respective school. It is required for getting NOC Stamp on RP/RC from FRO/FRRO.

     Documents required: 

        i. Copies of Passport & Visa with extensions if any. 

        ii. Copies of RC & RP + Original. 

        iii. NOC for travelling in original. 

        iv. Flight ticket copy 

Student must check the following before travelling:- 

        a) Students must be back in India within the validity period of Visa. 

        b) It will be more easier and convenient, if required to go out of India during their study period, that if the student comes with Multiple Entry Visa. Students must take with them Bonafide Certificate for the purpose of availing New Visa before going out of India. Student should approach Admission and Facilitation Department for this purpose.
