Admission Process

STEP 1         Submit online application form, supporting all relevant information, at www.eduzina.com . A profile will be created and you will be mailed your login credentials.

STEP 2        Wait until you receive offer of admission from our university. A message will be sent to you to intimate announcement of result on your MY APPLICATION STATUS.

STEP 3        After receiving the offer letter complete the condition mentioned in the offer letter.

STEP 4       On completion of condition as describe in the offer letter. The admisssion letter and visa related document will be send to your register email.

STEP 5        Verification follows with complete fees deposition.

STEP 6        Attend orientation and confirm enrolment.

STEP 7        Start classes, take advantage of campus opportunities and transform your future into a bright one.
