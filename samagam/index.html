About Samagam

SAMAGAM is the annual techno-cultural festival of the
Ram Krishna Dharmarth Foundation University.
It is a 3 days festival including technical & cultural events, a time when RKDF University comes alive with life, music & dance and most importantly with unforgettable memories that are created here.
